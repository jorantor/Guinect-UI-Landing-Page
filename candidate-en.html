FAQs

1
What is Güinect for?
Güinect helps communities and public representatives stay connected, organize local knowledge, and use that information to support more informed public decisions.
2
Why does local experience matter?
People who live in a place know things that rarely show up in a report: what works, what does not, what has already been tried, who can help, and where opportunities are. That lived experience adds context that is hard to get from outside the community.
3
How can neighbors take part?
Neighbors can share opinions, report problems, contribute ideas and local knowledge, answer consultations, suggest solutions, prioritize needs, connect with one another, and organize around issues they care about.
4
What can I learn about my community?
Güinect helps surface knowledge that is usually spread across hundreds or thousands of people: causes behind local problems, solutions already tried, technical knowledge, existing resources and capabilities, key local actors, emerging ideas, and the gap between what is assumed to be happening and what residents actually experience.
5
Can Güinect be used during a campaign?
Yes. Güinect can support community listening during a campaign through diagnostics, topic-based consultations, collaborative proposals, and dashboards that organize what people share.
6
What happens to the community network after a campaign?
The network can stay active so people can keep contributing, following commitments, and connecting around local issues over time.
7
How is participation organized and analyzed?
Participation can be grouped into themes, priorities, geographic patterns, ideas, and reports so large volumes of input are easier to review.
8
How do I get started?
Book a demo and tell us about the community, goals, and questions you want to understand. We can show how the tools can be configured for that context.
